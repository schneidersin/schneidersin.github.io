Hamster Roll 3D Third-Party Notices

Last updated: 17 November 2026
For the game Hamster Roll 3D. Back to the Terms of Service.

Hamster Roll 3D is made with open-source software, software libraries from Google and a free font, and its music was made with Suno. Thank you to everyone who made them. Their authors ask for the notices below to come with every copy of the game.

The same notices are in the game: tap Licences at the bottom of SETTINGS (or LICENCES on the ACCOUNT screen).

Part 1 lists what is in the game: each part, its copyright line and its licence. Part 2 has the full text of each licence, once. Part 3 lists the open-source parts inside Google's libraries in the Android app, as Google lists them.

  1. What is in the game
  2. The licences
  3. The open-source parts inside Google's libraries (the Android app)
